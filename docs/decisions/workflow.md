## Issue #292 decisions

### ISSUE-292-DEC-001 — Competitor research with selected requirements handoff

- Status: confirmed owner instruction, research workflow only.
- Source: project session, 2026-11-25. The owner agreed to a reusable competitor
  research role, asked for a handoff after consultation to an agent that writes
  requirements/issues, and then instructed: "ok, dann erstelle den agenten"
  (English: "okay, then create the agent"). The preceding proposal identified
  wled_researcher and reuse of wled_requirements through the coordinator.
- Decision: add the research role with repository-backed context and dated
  evidence. Present concrete proposals; only explicitly owner-selected scope is
  handed to requirements for scoped documentation and issue creation/update.
  That selection does not start product implementation.
- Scope: role setup and the research-to-requirements process. No competitor
  feature, automatic schedule, installation, hardware action, merge, deployment
  or publication is authorized by this instruction. Previously undocumented
  research preferences remain unknown; comparison criteria are working defaults.
- References: [issue #292](https://github.com/descipar/wled-mcp/issues/292),
  [research brief](../RESEARCH.md).
